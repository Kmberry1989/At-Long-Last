# Connect Four finale and collection assets

Generated master artwork for the Connect Four finale. The app uses compressed WebP derivatives in `public/assets/connect-four/` so phones do not need to download the full-size PNG masters.

- `board-concept.png`: empty 7-column by 6-row heirloom-style board, burgundy velvet, mahogany, and antique brass.
- `player-heart-token.png`: rose enamel and heart piece.
- `player-crescent-token.png`: midnight teal enamel and crescent piece.

The original two token masters remain as first-pass concept references. The current game uses three generated, face-on nine-token atlases (`token-atlas.png`, `token-atlas-2.png`, and `token-atlas-3.png`) and optimized WebP copies. The board art is a visual shell; the 7×6 spaces and falling pieces are interactive UI layered on top.

The 27 token motifs are garnet heart, crescent moon, strawberry, and little chicken (available immediately), then leaf at 3 shared nights, key at 7, shell at 14, lantern at 25, letter at 40, blossom at 60, compass at 100, candle at 125, teacup at 150, cottage at 175, paintbox at 200, hourglass at 225, umbrella at 250, picnic basket at 275, music box at 300, sunrise hills at 365, honey jar at 400, cherries at 425, fern at 450, moonlit window at 475, apple at 500, ribbon bow at 525, and garden lantern at 550. Unlocks are shared couple milestones, never cost hearts, and are cosmetic only. Each player chooses one token in Profile before the match; both choices must differ, and every piece they place in that match uses that token.
