# At Long Last — Future Asset Wishlist

## Highest-impact next additions

1. **A signature dice set** — Two soft enamel dice, one rose and one blue, with
   readable pips and a short tumble animation.
2. **Keepsakes that appear on the board** — A sparkler photo, midnight snack,
   folded love note, and last-dance ticket matching the keepsakes already earned
   during play.
3. **A central date-night vignette** — A small picnic blanket or café table that
   gives the open middle of the board a memorable focal point.
4. **Animated candles and string lights** — Gentle flame and glow movement would
   make the board feel alive without competing with gameplay.
5. **Player-piece bases** — Small rose/blue enamel bases or rings that keep every
   collectible readable against the grass, regardless of its original colors.

## Motion and effects

- Dice roll and landing animation
- Piece hop, squash, and landing shadow
- Heart trail between spaces
- Tile pulse for the active destination
- Candle flicker and firefly particles
- Confetti, petals, or sparklers for the finale
- Soft page-turn transition when opening the scrapbook

## Asset delivery guidelines

- GLB with Draco-compressed geometry and embedded WebP textures
- Bottom-centered pivot, Y-up, and a clean default rotation
- Keep player pieces near a one-unit bounding box
- Prefer 256–512 px textures and roughly 150 KB or less per player piece
- Include a clear human-readable filename with no spaces
- Provide a square transparent preview render for every selectable piece
- Avoid extremely thin details that disappear on a phone-sized board
